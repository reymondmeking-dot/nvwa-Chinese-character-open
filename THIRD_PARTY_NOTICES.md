# Third-party notices

The repository contains third-party font software. Those files are not relicensed under the repository MIT license.

- The complete distribution table is in [`plugins/chinese-character-open/FONT_LICENSES.md`](plugins/chinese-character-open/FONT_LICENSES.md).
- Every bundled font has its upstream license text under `plugins/chinese-character-open/fonts/<id>-<slug>/licenses/`.
- The four link-only entries contain no font binary.
- Preview PNGs are rendered works made with the listed fonts; they do not contain downloadable or extractable font software.

When redistributing an exported font, preserve the license directory generated beside it.
