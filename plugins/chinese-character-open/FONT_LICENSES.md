## Rules for bundled assets

- Do not remove or replace license files.
- Do not treat the collection as being under a single license.
- Do not subset, convert, rename, or modify the IPA-licensed assets `10` and `20` in place.
- Do not add binaries for the four link-only entries without new, explicit redistribution permission from the rightsholder.
- Keep `catalog/fonts.json` hashes in sync with any upstream version update.
